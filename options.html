Privacy

What GroupPing reads
  • Direct (1-to-1) chats: never. They are never opened, read, stored or shown - not even their message previews. A chat GroupPing cannot positively identify as a group is skipped.
  • Groups you switch on: new messages, so GroupPing can find plans, deadlines, decisions and questions for you.
  • All other groups: only the name, last-active time and unread count from your chat list - enough to list them here and rank reply reminders. Their message text is never stored.
What is stored, and where
  • Everything lives in this browser's extension storage. Nothing goes to any GroupPing server - there isn't one.
  • Messages are kept a few days at most, and never past your "how far back to look" window (Settings > General, default 30 days).
  • Before anything is stored, OTPs, bank/card alerts and credentials are dropped whole, and codes, card/account/Aadhaar/PAN numbers, phone numbers, emails and payment links inside other messages are masked (like <code>). This guard is pattern matching - very good, but not a legal guarantee.
What is sent to Google Gemini, and when
  • Only redacted text of NEW messages from your switched-on groups, at scan time (hourly or every 3 hours), using YOUR own Gemini API key. Chats with nothing plan-like are never sent.
  • Free Gemini keys: Google says it may use free-tier API content to improve its products. A paid (pay-as-you-go) key is not used for training. If that matters to you, use a paid key or Local-only mode below.
Local-only mode
No Gemini calls at all. GroupPing stops analysing messages; reply reminders (which use only chat names and times) keep working. No API key needed.

Your data

Deletes every message, plan, summary, setting, license key and log GroupPing has. The extension itself stays installed.

General

If Google reports high demand (503), GroupPing retries, then falls back to gemini-3.5-flash-lite.
Plus a final scan every evening. Only messages never analysed before are sent to Gemini, and chats with nothing plan-like cost nothing.
Older chats are never opened, read or sent to Gemini, and anything stored from before this window is deleted. The chat-list scroll stops as soon as it reaches older chats.
Open groups with new activity to read them
Needed to read groups you are not looking at. WhatsApp marks those chats as read and senders may see blue ticks. Off = GroupPing only reads the chat you have open.

Debug log

Self-test checks every reader against your live WhatsApp page (structure and counts only - never message text) and writes the result to this log. Open a group in WhatsApp first for the full check.
